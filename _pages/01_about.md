<p>I'm a Staff Software Engineer at Cisco Slido, where I have been since March 2022. My focus has shifted progressively from writing code to leading the people and processes around it — acting as Tech Lead shadow, running cross-functional delivery across Engineering, Product and Design, mentoring engineers, and taking ownership of architecture decisions and delivery risk.</p>

<p>Before Cisco, I spent a year at Sword Group as Team Lead and Tech Lead on the eu-LISA Interoperability programme — a greenfield initiative connecting the EU's large-scale border and security systems against a fixed, non-negotiable deadline. That role involved leading a team weighted toward junior engineers, coordinating across partner companies, and acting as the technical connection point between programme managers, architects, and developers.</p>

<p>Earlier I built and optimised trading-system infrastructure at Hellenic Exchanges (ATHEX), worked on heavily regulated platforms for the European Chemicals Agency (ECHA), and contributed to the Europass family of tools.</p>
